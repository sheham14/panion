What Panion does

Everything you need to shop smarter.

Built for people who buy groceries every week and want to stop guessing which store is cheapest.

📊

Price Comparison

See the current price of any product at every store side by side. Includes unit pricing so you can compare different pack sizes fairly.

🔔

Price Alerts

Add products to your watchlist and set a target price. Get notified by email when the price drops below your threshold.

🛒

Smart Shopping Lists

Build grocery lists and see the estimated total per store automatically. Know your cheapest run before you leave home.

🥫

Pantry Tracker

Log what's in your pantry with quantities and expiry dates. See at a glance what's running low or about to expire.

🍳

Recipe Manager

Save recipes with ingredients linked to real products. Panion calculates the cost per serving and tells you which store is cheapest to cook from.

✨

Clove — AI Assistant

Ask Clove for meal ideas based on what's on sale, what's in your pantry, or your dietary needs. Powered by Claude AI.

How it works

Up and running in two minutes.

No downloads, no setup. Open the app, sign in, and you're done.

1

Sign in with Google

One tap — no password to create or remember. Your data is tied to your Google account.

2

Pick your stores

Choose which stores you actually shop at. Panion focuses price comparisons on what's relevant to you.

3

Add products to your watchlist

Search for the items you buy regularly. Panion starts tracking prices and alerts you to drops.

4

Shop smarter every week

Check your dashboard before you go. See where your list is cheapest this week and head to the right store.
